# Security policy

Zorko runs untrusted web pages and passes their content to AI agents, so prompt-injection bypasses, ways to make Zorko type secrets, skip `!confirm`, reach the local network, or leak data from the journal are all security issues.

Please report them privately: open the repository's **Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**. Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability.

Include the Zorko version, your OS, and a page or steps that reproduce the problem. You will get a reply within a few days.
